\section{Future Enhancements}
While ScholarFlow successfully delivers its comprehensive core feature set for paper management, academic writing, and real-time collaboration, several strategic enhancements are planned to further elevate the platform for broader institutional adoption:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Native Mobile Applications}: While the current web platform is fully responsive down to 375\,px viewports, developing dedicated iOS and Android mobile apps will enable offline paper reading and mobile PDF annotations on tablets with stylus support.
\item \textbf{Enterprise Identity and Institutional Billing}: Integration with SAML 2.0 / Single Sign-On (SSO) providers, campus-wide site licenses, centralized domain verification, and institutional procurement invoicing.
\item \textbf{Advanced AI Knowledge Synthesis}: Cross-paper literature gap detection, automated citation graph anomaly analysis, and thematic trend clustering across multi-thousand paper libraries.
\item \textbf{Ecosystem Plugins and Public API}: Developing browser extensions for one-click paper captures from publisher websites, along with bi-directional add-ins for Overleaf, Microsoft Word, and LaTeX editors.
\item \textbf{Offline-First Collaboration with CRDT Persistence}: Extending the Y.js collaboration engine with local IndexedDB caching to enable uninterrupted writing during intermittent internet connectivity with automatic conflict-free reconciliation upon reconnecting.
\item \textbf{Multilingual Support and Localization}: Broadening user interface localization and multilingual AI query processing to support academic researchers across non-English research repositories.
\end{itemize}
